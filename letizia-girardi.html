Letizia Girardi

Ph.D. Student in Computer Science @ University of Trento

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Trento, Italy, with a strong focus on medical imaging, deep learning, and AI-driven diagnostics. My research bridges computational modeling, multimodal data integration, and clinical applications, aiming to create robust and interpretable AI tools for healthcare.

I hold a Master's Degree in Computer Science (Bioinformatics) from the University of Trento. Prior to that, I earned a Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science with a thesis on detection and localization of deep video inpainting. I also completed an exchange semester at Charles University in Prague, exploring international research perspectives in computational sciences.

🔬 Primary Research Interests:

Longitudinal Multimodal Learning in Healthcare: multimodal deep-learning modeling integrating medical imaging with clinical data. Focus on generative AI models for temporal morphological analysis to quantify anatomical progression and improve diagnostic generalization.

Multimodal Fusion Longitudinal Modeling Generative AI Disease Progression Simulation Disease Trajectory

🌱 When I'm not doing research, you'll probably find me travelling, doing pilates, or walking somewhere with a podcast on.
